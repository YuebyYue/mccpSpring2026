# Recording Notes

Use this as note cards only. Do not read it aloud as a script.

## 3-minute route

0:00-0:25 - Hook

- Ultrasound is not just a picture.
- It is a live skill: hand, eye, screen, patient.
- A good scan depends on experience.

0:25-0:55 - Problem

- New users may not know what they are seeing.
- They may not know if the image is clear enough.
- They may miss an important view.

0:55-1:25 - Aim

- Proposed direction: AI co-pilot for ultrasound.
- Not replacing clinicians.
- Helping users during the scan.

1:25-2:05 - How it works, in simple words

- Watches ultrasound video.
- Listens to voice or questions.
- Follows probe movement.
- Uses reports and medical knowledge.

2:05-2:40 - What it can guide

- What am I seeing?
- Is this view clear?
- What did I miss?
- Where should I move?

2:40-3:00 - Significance

- Shorter learning curve.
- More consistent scans.
- Better access to skilled care.
- Closing phrase: AI as a guide beside the human, not a replacement.
